Privacy policy

Toss for iPhone and the Toss computer app · Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version

Toss doesn't collect any data. What you share goes directly from your iPhone to a computer you paired yourself, over your local network. It never passes through our servers. There are none.

What happens to what you share

  • Items you share with Toss (text, links, photos, videos, files) are sent from your iPhone to your paired computer over an encrypted connection (TLS). The iPhone only accepts the computer whose certificate matches the pairing code you scanned.
  • On the computer, files are saved in the folder you chose (by default Downloads/From iPhone) and text or links may be copied to the clipboard. They stay there under your control.
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere else, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What is stored on your devices

  • iPhone: the list of paired computers (name, network addresses, certificate fingerprint and an access key) and your settings, in the iPhone's keychain. Removing a computer in the app or deleting the app removes them.
  • Computer: the list of paired phones (name, an ID, and a hash of each phone's access key), settings and a log of received items' names, in Toss's settings folder.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code. No images are stored or sent.
  • Local network: to connect to your computer.
  • Photos: Toss sees only the photos you pick or share with it.
